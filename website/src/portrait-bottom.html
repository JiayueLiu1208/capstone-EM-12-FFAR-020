Real-Time Robotic Portrait Drawing System

By Jacqueline Mei, Luna Liu, Yi Ji

Supervised by Farhad Farokhi

A live capstone experience

Your face. Drawn live.

Sit for a photo. Watch technology turn your expression into a one-of-a-kind line portrait.

Imagine taking a photo and watching a robot turn it into a drawing right in front of you. Our system transforms digital images into sketch paths, then guides a robotic arm to recreate the portrait on paper as a unique physical artwork.

System pipeline

From photo to physical artwork

  1. Photo capture

    A ring light and camera capture a clear portrait in real time.

  2. Image processing

    The computer finds the lines: face regions become edges and semantic layers.

  3. Sketch conversion

    The photo becomes a few essential strokes: eyes, contour, hair.

  4. Drawing path

    The strokes are put in order as one robot-ready path.

  5. Robot planning

    The path is turned into motion for the arm.

  6. Control

    The controller drives the 3-DOF arm along the path.

  7. Robot drawing

    A real pen follows the plotted path across a take-home postcard, one line at a time.
