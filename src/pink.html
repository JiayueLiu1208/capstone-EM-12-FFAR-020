hello, this is

Placeholder, a tiny robot arm that writes in gold ink.

It is a four-joint desk robot. The base turns to face each letter, the shoulder and elbow lean in and out, and a little wrist keeps the pen pointing straight down at the paper.

Both arm links are exactly 150 mm long, so the arm folds like a pair of compasses: seen from above, the elbow always sits right in the middle between the base and the pen. The arm at the top of this page runs the same maths as the real one.

step by step

How it writes

  1. You type a word

    In the box under the desk, or on the command line.

  2. Letters become strokes

    Each letter is a few single pen strokes, the way you would write it.

  3. Plan the route

    Strokes are ordered so the pen hops as little as possible, then split every half millimetre.

  4. Solve the joints

    Every point turns into four angles: base, shoulder, elbow and wrist.

  5. Write!

    Four servos get new angles 50 times a second. Between strokes the arm lifts the pen 8 mm.

the maths is cute too

Two equal links, one triangle

Because the links match, shoulder, elbow and wrist always make an isosceles triangle, and a single arccos solves it. The pen hangs as far below the wrist as the shoulder sits above the table, so while it writes the wrist stays level with the shoulder.

θ₁ = atan2(x, y)
β = arccos(√(r² + h²) / 2L), r = √(x² + y²), h = z
θ₂ = atan2(h, r) + β, θ₃ = −2β, θ₄ = −90° − θ₂ − θ₃
θ₁ base0°
θ₂ shoulder0°
θ₃ elbow0°
θ₄ wrist0°

The pen is at x 0, y 0, z 0 mm

what it's made of

Parts

  • 3 × MG996R servos base, shoulder, elbow
  • 1 × MG90S servo wrist
  • Arduino Nano
  • PCA9685 servo board
  • 2 printed links 150 mm each
  • Printed turret and pen clip
  • 51107 thrust bearing + 4 × 608ZZ
  • 6 V, 5 A power supply
  • Gold gel pen
dear diary

Build diary

  1. 4 Sep

    Picked a four-joint arm with two equal 150 mm links. The reach study says it covers a whole A4 sheet.

  2. 18 Sep

    Printed the links and the turret. Calibrated all four servos with a protractor; they are about 2° off at the ends of their travel.

  3. 25 Sep

    Gave the servos their own 6 V supply after the Arduino kept resetting halfway through a letter.

  4. 2 Oct

    Font, planner and maths working together for the first time. It wrote its first, very wobbly, word.

  5. 16 Oct

    Fixed the wobble at the elbow. Now it writes neatly enough to sign its own website.
